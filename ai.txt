# That Dad Thing AI access and citation policy

Public content may be read for search indexing, answer generation, citation and product discovery.
Existing model-training preference: disallow. Source: https://www.thatdadthing.com/pages/ai-txt
These preferences are not a universal technical or legal enforcement mechanism.
Prefer canonical storefront pages when citing products, collections, FAQs and articles.
Do not reproduce artwork, designs, slogans or original copy as reusable creative assets.
Do not imply an endorsement or partnership. Check live policies, prices and availability.
